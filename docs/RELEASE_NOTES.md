# Know Your Constellation v3.12

Reveals mission constellations and enemy forecasts on the war table and briefing screen so you can choose your loadout before deployment.

- Local forecasts for hosted operation missions and other players' joinable missions.
- Terminid, Automaton and Illuminate constellation names and associated units.
- Scrolling reports using the game's body font and matching panel style.
- Alternate Rows download lists every forecast section at once with the same font, frame and visibility rules. Both layouts were verified in-game.
- Briefing visibility follows the native panel and stays hidden on loadout.
- Requires Bingus Shared Loader v12 or newer, installed separately.
- Does not change spawns or affect players without the mod. Spawns are not guaranteed.

Includes the in-game confirmed v3.12 operation preview fix.
